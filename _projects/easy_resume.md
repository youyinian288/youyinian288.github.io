Easy Resume 简历助手是一款面向求职者的 iOS 简历工具，支持简历制作与内容优化，帮助用户更高效地整理个人经历、完善求职材料，并生成更专业的简历。

Easy Resume is an iOS resume tool for job seekers. It helps users organize their experience, improve their applications, and create clearer, more professional resumes.
